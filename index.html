Three made-up supplier invoices for one pub. Plain code, no model. What it cannot settle, the manager decides.

Checking

A line is ready when its sum adds up, its code is one item on the stock list, the size it is written in is that item's size, it is charged in the unit you buy it in, its VAT code matches your list, goods in counted what was charged, and the price is the last cost on your list.

Cases become bottles, bags or each by the pack on your list. Kegs and casks show the cost of a pint, spirits the cost of a 25ml measure. Anything else waits for the manager with the reason and the choices.
